# modulariti.ai

## What it is
An engineering studio that solves business problems with complete, production-grade
systems — using AI, automation, data and cloud engineering as the tools. The systems
run on the client's own infrastructure.

## The surface this covers
A pre-launch coming-soon page. Launch date: 1 October 2026 (PKT).

## Audience
Organisations held back by broken process and data nobody is using — not by a lack of
technology. Typically a buyer who has already watched an AI pilot stall before production.

## What the visitor must believe
- Their data never leaves their environment.
- Most AI projects fail on readiness, not algorithms — and this studio addresses readiness.
- Someone is accountable for the outcome, and the client's own team ends up owning the system.

## The action
Leave an email address to be notified at launch.

## Unique mechanism
AI assembled from composable parts — models, agents, and pipelines — deployed inside the
client's own boundary rather than behind a vendor API.

## What we build
AI systems · Automation & integration · Data engineering & analytics ·
Cloud & delivery infrastructure · EU AI Act readiness

## What we are not
Not an AI vendor. Not a staffing supplier. Not advice for someone else to implement.

## Brand commitments (pinned by the user, not inferred)
- White theme, Meta's corporate blue (#0866FF) as the single primary. Not the Meta
  blue→purple rebrand gradient.
- IBM Plex Sans throughout; IBM Plex Mono only for measured values.
- Corporate and trustworthy register.
- Reference feel: gethookd.ai — modern SaaS marketing polish, soft elevated material,
  generous padding, sticky nav.
- The scroll-driven MacBook demo is an approved fixture of the page.
